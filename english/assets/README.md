## Forest prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a production game background asset for a Korean children's top-down block/voxel action RPG. Landscape 16:9 or approximately 900x520 ratio, completely fills canvas. No text, UI, characters, monsters, chests, items or logos. Fixed orthographic near-top-down camera, NO horizon, no panoramic vista, no tilted isometric diamond board. A beautiful inviting enchanted woodland clearing framed by dense cubic treetops, angular mossy stones and tiny ferns ONLY within the outermost 4% border. The inner 92% width and 84% height must be fully open walkable FLAT meadow with a subtle winding EARTH PATH from left center to right center and a small flush ancient stone mosaic near center. NO solid objects, trees, obstacles or water inside the play area. All interior details must look flat so characters can walk everywhere. Crisp deliberately painted pixel-art/voxel-game textures, tasteful fine detail in border foliage, layered canopy shading, little golden flowers along edges, moss colored ground tiles subtly varied. Enchanting soft warm sunlight through trees with diagonal dappled shadows, subdued emerald and sage greens with ocher paths. Moderate lighting, never too dark. Clear quiet low-contrast center for small bright block characters and yellow/red danger markers to read easily. A polished indie-game art look more detailed and rich than plain rectangles, yet coherent with low-resolution block characters. Environment artwork ONLY, no screenshot mockup. Brand-new original art.

## Lava prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Production background texture for a children's top-down block/voxel action RPG. Landscape 16:9 about 900x520 ratio. Entire canvas is an orthographic near-top-down lava cave arena. NO text, UI, characters, objects to collect, monsters, chests, sky, horizon or perspective distance. Every inner part of the arena is flat walkable cooled basalt, dark desaturated mauve and warm slate-gray stone slabs. A subtle flat ocher ancient geometric mosaic in the very center and broad flat weathered path from left center to right center. All lava rivers, solid tall rock formations, glowing crystals and cubic ruins are constrained to the outermost 4% border, NOT inside the inner 92% width and 84% height playfield. Border has beautiful orange molten lava, angular dark rock pillars with warm reflected lighting and a few gold ember flecks. The inner arena must be quiet and low contrast, medium-dark so small bright block characters, red circular boss danger and yellow arrow lines remain highly readable; never put bright lava cracks across the walkable play area as those would look like damaging terrain. Crisp detailed painted pixel-art with voxel geometry. Polished inviting indie game art, charming magical adventure mood suitable for 7-8 year olds, not scary, no skeletons. Rich material texture and atmospheric edge lighting, not a flat diagram, not a 3D isometric diamond island. Background artwork only.
